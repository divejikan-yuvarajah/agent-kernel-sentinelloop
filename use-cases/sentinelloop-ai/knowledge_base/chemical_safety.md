# Chemical Safety

## Immediate worker actions
- Move away from a chemical spill, leak, strong smell, or visible vapour.
- Avoid touching the chemical or contaminated surfaces.
- Warn nearby workers to stay away from the affected area.
- Do not try to clean or contain the chemical unless you are trained to do so.
- Avoid spreading contamination to other areas.
- Report the chemical hazard immediately through the site safety process.
- Keep the area clear until trained personnel arrive.

## Site regulations
- Chemicals must be labelled, stored in approved containers, and kept in designated stores.
- Keep acids, oxidisers, flammables, and incompatibles separated as posted on store signs.
- Safety data information must be available for substances used in the area.
- Spill kits, eyewash, and emergency showers must remain unobstructed and inspected.
- Eating, drinking, and storing food in chemical work areas is not permitted.
- Decanting or mixing chemicals is allowed only for trained staff following the SOP.
- Waste chemicals must go to labelled waste containers, never to drains or open ground.
- Ventilation must stay on in storage and process areas while chemicals are in use.
- Report leaking drums, missing labels, or damaged secondary containment at once.
- Contractors may handle site chemicals only under the same storage and PPE rules.

## Worker duties
- Wear the chemical PPE required for the task, including gloves, goggles, and apron.
- If skin or eyes are exposed, use the eyewash or shower and call for medical help.
- Remove contaminated clothing carefully and do not take it into clean areas.
- Stay upwind of vapours and leave if you feel dizzy, nauseous, or short of breath.
- Tell the supervisor which substance was involved if the label is known.

## Prohibited actions
- Do not taste, smell closely, or touch unknown liquids or powders.
- Do not use bare hands, rags, or unofficial bottles to move chemicals.
- Do not hose a spill into walkways, drains, or other work bays.
- Do not neutralize or mix spilled chemicals unless the approved procedure says so.

Always follow instructions from trained safety or emergency personnel.
