# Chemical Safety

- Move away from a chemical spill, leak, strong smell, or visible vapour.
- Avoid touching the chemical or contaminated surfaces.
- Warn nearby workers to stay away from the affected area.
- Do not try to clean or contain the chemical unless you are trained to do so.
- Avoid spreading contamination to other areas.
- Report the chemical hazard immediately through the site safety process.
- Keep the area clear until trained personnel arrive.

Always follow instructions from trained safety or emergency personnel.
