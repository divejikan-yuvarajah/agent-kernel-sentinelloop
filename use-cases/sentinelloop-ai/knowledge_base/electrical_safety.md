# Electrical Safety

- Keep away from exposed wires, sparks, smoke, or damaged electrical equipment.
- Do not touch the damaged equipment, cables, or nearby metal parts.
- Warn nearby workers to stay away from the dangerous area.
- Stop using the equipment only if this can be done without approaching the hazard.
- Keep water and wet materials away from the electrical danger.
- Report the hazard immediately through the site safety process.
- Keep the area clear until it is made safe.

Always follow instructions from trained safety or emergency personnel.
