# Electrical Safety

## Immediate worker actions
- Keep away from exposed wires, sparks, smoke, or damaged electrical equipment.
- Do not touch the damaged equipment, cables, or nearby metal parts.
- Warn nearby workers to stay away from the dangerous area.
- Stop using the equipment only if this can be done without approaching the hazard.
- Keep water and wet materials away from the electrical danger.
- Report the hazard immediately through the site safety process.
- Keep the area clear until it is made safe.

## Site regulations
- Only authorized electrical personnel may isolate, inspect, test, or repair electrical systems.
- Do not restore power until a competent person confirms isolation and the area is safe.
- Electrical rooms, panels, and substations must stay locked and access-controlled.
- Keep a clear approach in front of every distribution board and control panel.
- Do not store tools, pallets, drums, or combustible materials in electrical rooms.
- Damaged cords, cracked plugs, missing covers, and overloaded sockets must be taken out of use.
- Temporary wiring is not a substitute for a permanent, inspected supply.
- Work on or near live parts is prohibited unless you are trained, authorized, and protected.
- Report buzzing, burning smell, overheating, tripping breakers, or flickering lights at once.
- Outdoor or wet-area equipment must be treated as higher risk until confirmed dry and intact.

## Worker duties
- Treat every unknown cable as live until a competent person says otherwise.
- Use only inspected tools and PPE assigned for electrical work.
- Do not wear metal jewellery or wet clothing when near exposed conductors.
- Follow lock-out and tag-out instructions posted by the electrical team.
- Stay at a safe distance from overhead lines, transformers, and welding supplies.
- Guide visitors and contractors away from the electrical hazard until it is controlled.

## Prohibited actions
- Do not attempt DIY repairs, jumper connections, or bypassing of safety devices.
- Do not use water, foam, or wet cloths on electrical fires or live equipment.
- Do not pull cables, open panels, or reset breakers unless you are authorized.
- Do not leave an electrical hazard unmarked or unreported at shift change.

Always follow instructions from trained safety or emergency personnel.
