# Fire and Smoke Safety

- Move away from flames, heavy smoke, or increasing heat.
- Warn nearby workers and follow the site's emergency alert process.
- Use the nearest safe exit if the area is becoming dangerous.
- Do not enter or return to a smoke-filled or burning area.
- Keep away from equipment, fuel, or materials involved in the fire.
- Go to the designated safe area if evacuation is required.
- Report anyone who may still be inside the affected area.

Always follow instructions from trained safety or emergency personnel.
