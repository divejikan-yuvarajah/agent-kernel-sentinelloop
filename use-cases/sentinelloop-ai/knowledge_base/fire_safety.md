# Fire and Smoke Safety

## Immediate worker actions
- Move away from flames, heavy smoke, or increasing heat.
- Warn nearby workers and follow the site's emergency alert process.
- Use the nearest safe exit if the area is becoming dangerous.
- Do not enter or return to a smoke-filled or burning area.
- Keep away from equipment, fuel, or materials involved in the fire.
- Go to the designated safe area if evacuation is required.
- Report anyone who may still be inside the affected area.

## Site regulations
- Keep evacuation routes, fire doors, and assembly points clear at all times.
- Do not block fire extinguishers, hose reels, alarm call points, or emergency exits.
- Combustible waste, packaging, and oily rags must be stored in approved containers.
- Hot work such as welding or grinding requires a permit and a fire watch where required.
- Fuel, solvents, and gas cylinders must be stored away from ignition sources.
- Do not use lifts during a fire or smoke emergency unless directed by emergency staff.
- Close fire doors behind you; do not wedge them open.
- Report damaged alarms, missing extinguishers, or blocked exits immediately.
- Smoking is allowed only in designated outdoor areas, never near flammables.
- After an alarm, do not re-enter until the all-clear is given by the emergency team.

## Worker duties
- Know your nearest exit, secondary exit, and assembly point before starting work.
- Raise the alarm as soon as fire or smoke is seen, even if it looks small.
- Assist others to leave only if you can do so without entering the danger zone.
- Account for your work group at the assembly point and report missing persons.
- Follow the site emergency plan and any instructions from wardens or officers.

## Prohibited actions
- Do not fight a developing, spreading, or electrical fire unless you are trained to do so.
- Do not hide, disable, or ignore fire alarms.
- Do not go back for tools, phones, or personal belongings during evacuation.
- Do not use water on oil, chemical, or electrical fires.

Always follow instructions from trained safety or emergency personnel.
