# General Workplace Hazards

- Keep a safe distance from the hazard.
- Warn nearby workers about the dangerous condition.
- Stop using unsafe equipment if this can be done safely.
- Do not enter an area that looks unstable or unsafe.
- Keep people away from spills, damaged equipment, or blocked paths.
- Report the hazard immediately through the site safety process.
- Wait until the area or equipment has been confirmed safe before returning.

Always follow instructions from trained safety or emergency personnel.
