# Machine Safety

## Immediate worker actions
- Never bypass, remove, or reach around a machine guard while equipment is running.
- Keep emergency-stop controls clear and easy to reach.
- Stop the machine before clearing a jam, adjusting a part, or retrieving material.
- Stay clear of rotating, cutting, or pinch-point equipment.
- Warn nearby workers if a guard is missing or a machine is behaving abnormally.
- Report the machine hazard immediately through the site safety process.
- Do not restart equipment until trained personnel confirm it is safe.

## Site regulations
- Machine guarding must remain in place during operation; missing guards are a stop-work condition.
- Only trained and authorized operators may start, set, or service production machinery.
- Isolation and lock-out are required before maintenance, cleaning inside the machine, or tool changes.
- Emergency stops, interlocks, and light curtains must not be bypassed or taped over.
- Abnormal noise, vibration, smoke, or unexpected movement requires an immediate stop and report.
- Floor markings and exclusion zones around CNC, presses, and conveyors must be respected.
- Loose clothing, jewellery, and unsecured hair are not permitted near rotating equipment.
- Compressed air must not be used to clean clothing or to blow chips toward people.
- Contractors working on machinery must follow the same isolation rules as site staff.
- After any incident or near miss, the machine stays locked out until released by a supervisor.

## Worker duties
- Inspect guards, e-stops, and workholding before starting a cycle.
- Keep hands, tools, and rags away from the point of operation.
- Use push sticks, holders, or approved fixtures instead of reaching into the machine.
- Tell the next shift if a machine was stopped for a safety reason.
- Stand clear of automatic start-up and robotic motion paths.

## Prohibited actions
- Do not reach into a machine to grab a part while it can still move.
- Do not operate machinery you have not been trained and signed off to use.
- Do not silence interlocks, jumper safety circuits, or remove covers for convenience.
- Do not restart after a jam until the cause is controlled and the guard is refitted.

Always follow instructions from trained safety or emergency personnel.
