# PPE Safety

## Immediate worker actions
- Wear the personal protective equipment required for the task and area.
- Replace damaged helmets, gloves, visors, or hearing protection immediately.
- Use hearing protection in CNC bays and other high-noise zones.
- Do not enter a restricted area without the PPE posted at the entry.
- Keep eye and face protection on when handling chemicals, sparks, or flying debris.
- Report missing or damaged PPE through the site safety process.
- Do not share damaged or contaminated PPE with another worker.

## Site regulations
- PPE requirements posted at area entry points are mandatory, not optional.
- Safety footwear, high-visibility clothing, and head protection apply in designated bays.
- Eye protection is required in machining, grinding, welding, and chemical handling areas.
- Hearing protection is required wherever noise signage or high-noise equipment is present.
- Respiratory protection is used only with fit, training, and the filter specified for the hazard.
- Damaged, expired, or contaminated PPE must be withdrawn and replaced.
- Visitors and contractors must wear the same area PPE as site workers.
- PPE storage must be clean and dry; do not leave helmets or gloves on oily floors.
- Supervisors must not allow work to start if required PPE is missing.
- Records of issue and replacement support incident and audit reviews.

## Worker duties
- Inspect PPE before each use for cracks, tears, missing straps, or poor seal.
- Wear PPE for the whole task, including short jobs and walk-throughs.
- Clean and store reusable PPE after use according to the area instruction.
- Ask for the correct size or type if the issued PPE does not fit safely.
- Report skin irritation, fogged visors, or equipment that prevents safe work.

## Prohibited actions
- Do not enter a PPE-required zone with helmets carried instead of worn.
- Do not modify PPE by cutting, painting, or drilling holes in it.
- Do not use another person's contaminated gloves, masks, or ear plugs.
- Do not treat PPE as a substitute for guarding, ventilation, or isolation.

Always follow instructions from trained safety or emergency personnel.
